Kraken Unleashed

A semester-long PBR animation production in Unreal Engine 5. I built the creature rig and produced the accompanying animation, VFX and environment work.

Role
Technical Artist
Year
2024
Tools
Unreal Engine 5, Maya, Substance
Context
Advanced Pipeline Topics, Carnegie Mellon ETC

Overview

The kraken's tentacles are the whole problem. Eight long, tapering limbs need to deform smoothly along their length, curl convincingly at the tip, and stay art-directable rather than simulated — an animator has to be able to pose them.

I built the rig around ribbon IK, which is also what drove me to write the ribbon IK plugin listed under Tools: the manual setup was too slow to iterate at the pace the production needed.

Contribution

  • Built and iterated the full creature rig through production.
  • Animated the creature and produced the accompanying VFX.
  • Built the underwater environment and lighting in Unreal Engine 5.
