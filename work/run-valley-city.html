About the work

Run – Valley City is an art game by Snow Yunxue Fu, on which I am credited as Studio Assistant.

It was exhibited in Let's Entertain, a group exhibition of digital and interactive work by 19 international multimedia artists, curated by RaidenInst, at Times Art Museum in Chongqing, China, in summer 2023.
