Erosion System

A runtime erosion effect in Unreal Engine with a blueprint-driven control panel for switching between modes and driving the erosion percentage.

Overview

An in-editor control panel exposes three erosion modes and a live percentage readout, so the effect can be dialled in against the character in the viewport rather than through trial-and-error recompiles.
